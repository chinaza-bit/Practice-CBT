PRACTICE CBT WEBSITE — SAMISTINTECH

This project is a student practice CBT portal, not an official examination.

COURSES
- ILS 212 — 350 practice questions in 5 batches of 70.
- GST 212 — 350 practice questions arranged as 10 chapter batches, with 35 questions per chapter.
- GET 204 — 350 practice questions (Engineering Workshop Practice) in 5 batches of 70, covering
  safety, measuring instruments, machine shop, hand tools & welding, foundry, casting, metal
  forming, heat treatment, material removal, machine tools, cutting theory & forces, CNC, and
  electrical/electronic components & circuits.
- IFT 212 (Computer Architecture & Organization) — a two-choice course page:
  1. Summary & Key Points notes (ift212_notes.html) — a full revision document covering all
     6 CCMAS topics with comparison tables (Von Neumann vs Harvard, x86 vs MIPS, hardwired vs
     microprogrammed control) and exam-style Q&A pulled from the course material.
  2. Practice CBT (ift212_exam.html) — 217 questions across 6 topic batches: Computer Hardware
     & ISA, Dataflow & Control Instructions, CPU Design, Assembly Language Programming, Memory
     Hierarchy, and I/O Organization.

GST 212 TEST SETUP
- Choose a practice mode: Normal or Batch.
- Normal mode: 70 questions selected from all 10 chapters (7 questions per chapter) with a fixed 40-minute general timer.
- Batch mode: select one of the 10 chapter batches. Each batch contains exactly 35 questions.
- Batch mode offers 5, 10 or 20 seconds per question, converted into one general countdown for the whole batch.
- The general countdown does not reset when moving between questions.
- When the general countdown reaches zero, the test is submitted automatically.
